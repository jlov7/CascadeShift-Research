# Disclaimer

CascadeShift is research software for a bounded synthetic prototype. It is not a production control, security product, compliance program, safety certification, or deployment recommendation.

The historical result concerns one local model/runtime, a synthetic corpus, a rule-only discovery interface, fixed condition order, and unsupported provider seeds. Its primary interval is inconclusive, and the discovery treatment did not expose a frozen/current difference in 14 of 30 shifted worlds. The project does not establish production failure rates, general model behavior, external validity, causal effects, independent replication, novelty, or a general rule that agents require current configuration access.

Do not supply production credentials, personal data, customer data, or sensitive configuration data to this prototype. Users remain responsible for security, privacy, legal obligations, and operational controls in their own environment.
