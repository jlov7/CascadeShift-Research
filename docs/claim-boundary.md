# Claim boundary

The v1 archive supports a narrow statement: in one synthetic corpus, one local model/runtime, and one rule-only discovery interface, the preregistered adjusted C2-versus-C1 estimate was +9.2 percentage points with a 95% interval from -1.7 to +20.8 percentage points. The result is inconclusive.

The archive checks can recompute that result, replay retained actions against the preserved engine, and show that C1 and C2 had no exposed discovery-response difference in 14 of 30 shifted worlds, including 9 of 15 plan-invalidating worlds. They do not rerun the historical model or independently implement the simulator.

The six measurement-v2 construction cases support a separate claim: the repaired development interface exposes declared configuration facts and validates witness plans and scoring fields. The stopped provider diagnostic supports no performance comparison, null result, or efficacy claim.

The project does not establish production safety, deployment suitability, enterprise failure rates, general model behavior, external validity, causal effects, independent replication, peer review, or priority over related benchmarks. See [Technical note](../TECHNICAL-NOTE.md).
