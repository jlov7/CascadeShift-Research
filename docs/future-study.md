# Future study proposal

This is a proposal for a new study. No larger study has been launched, preregistered, or promised.

The v1 archive is retained as historical evidence. A follow-up should use a new protocol because the v1 rule-only interface did not expose a frozen/current difference in 14 of 30 shifted worlds. The v2 six-case work is a development repair, not a substitute for that protocol.

## Stage 1: qualify the measurement

Use representative versions of all six cases and the full tool contract. Every selected case must have a declared action-relevant fact that differs between C1 and C2, accessible records and configuration facts in the relevant condition, and mechanically passing accessible-fact and safe-plan witnesses. The preflight must exercise typed calls, terminal completion, mixed response handling, and errors. Account for every record, action, and finish signal. A protocol-invalid record stops the stage; preserve its negative log before any rerun. Set fixed call, token, and cost limits in advance.

## Stage 2: run a feasibility pilot

Only after qualification passes, use a separate pilot to choose case clusters and a precision budget. It may test whether the measurement can complete under the stated limits, but it cannot make confirmatory efficacy claims. Report goal misses separately from hard-constraint failures. Predeclare exclusions and how invalid records are handled. A failed observability, witness, provider-contract, or accounting gate stops the pilot rather than expands the matrix.

## Stage 3: conduct a new confirmatory study

Only after the first two stages pass, preregister a multiworld, multimodel study with counterbalanced condition order, a cluster or unit of inference, fixed budgets and stop rules, and a target precision. Reconsider the primary estimand rather than carrying forward the v1 result automatically. A paired C2-minus-C1 contrast on shifted cases where the relevant fact is observable is one candidate. If a baseline-adjusted difference-in-differences estimand is retained, justify it prospectively and define parent weighting before collection. No sample size or result is promised here.

A null or negative result would be informative only if the protocol is valid and the measurement completes. This proposal makes no claim about the result of a future study.
